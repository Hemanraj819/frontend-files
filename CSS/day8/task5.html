Mahendra Singh Dhoni was born on July 7, 1981, in Ranchi, India. He rose from a small-town ticket collector for Indian Railways to become India's most successful and calmest cricket captain, winning all major ICC trophies and the 2011 World Cup

Virat Kohli’s inspiring story is one of grit, raw talent, and fierce discipline, taking him from the dusty cricket gullies of Delhi to becoming "King Kohli" and one of the greatest legends in cricket history.

Rohit Sharma's story is an inspiring journey of a boy from a modest background who overcame financial struggles to become one of the world's greatest cricket captains and record-breaking opening batsmen, famously known as the "Hitman"
