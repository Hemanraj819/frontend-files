About Kallakurichi

Kallakurichi became a separate district on 26 November 2019. It is known for its agriculture, especially sugarcane, rice, and groundnut cultivation.

Kallakurichi is a vibrant and emerging agricultural hub in Tamil Nadu, historically tied to the Chola Empire and nourished by the Gomuki River. Often celebrated as the "Home of Agriculture", the district thrives on extensive rice production, textile businesses, and sugar milling. It offers a unique blend of rural charm and natural beauty, featuring the scenic Kalvarayan Hills, multiple waterfalls, and ancient, culturally rich temples.Located strategically along the Chennai-Salem National Highway 79, Kallakurichi serves as an important junction in the state. Known officially as one of Tamil Nadu's newer districts since late 2019, it seamlessly combines modern connectivity with a rich traditional heritage. Visitors and locals alike are drawn to pristine spots like the Gomuki Dam and the cloud-touching peaks of Vellimalai during the monsoon seasons.
