Top IT Companies in India

The Indian IT sector is dominated by global technology and consulting powerhouses, with industry giants like TCS, Infosys, and Wipro leading the pack by market capitalization and revenue. Many of these firms drive digital transformation and global operations from major technology hubs.





Information Technology (IT) companies design, manage, and protect the digital infrastructure and software that modern businesses rely on to operate. They span both service-based providers (like Chennai-based Zoho Corporation or Mumbai-based TCS) and global product-based developers (like Microsoft and Amazon).IT companies drive global innovation and digital transformation by delivering specialized services across several core areas:Software Development: Building, testing, and maintaining custom web platforms, mobile applications, and enterprise software.Cloud & Data Services: Helping organizations securely migrate to the cloud and utilize artificial intelligence (AI) and data analytics to optimize decision-making.Cybersecurity: Protecting sensitive corporate and consumer data from malicious cyber threats and network vulnerabilities.IT Consulting & Management: Guiding businesses in restructuring their digital workflows and providing ongoing hardware and software support.



Tata consultancy services (TCS) is one of the top information technology – IT Company in India. The IT Company offers services & consulting services. It was found 1968. The Company headquarter was in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. It has largest campus and workforce in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. Now it operates from 149 locations across 46 countries. TCS provides the best service like mobile apps, Cloud Solutions, Block chain, Enterprise Apps, Custom Software Development, Automation and AI, the Internet of Things and many more. TCS is the largest company in the IT sector in the world, as of February 2021. By market capitalisation, TCS is the 2nd largest company. TCS is one of the most valuable IT services brand around the world. It was ranked at 64th position in the Forbes World’s Most Innovative Companies ranking in 2015. That made TCS the highest-ranked IT services company and the top Indian company. As per Fortune India 500 list in 2018, TCS ranked 11th. The company became the first Indian IT company to reach $100 billion in market capitalisation in 2018. TCS, Tata consultancy services & its subsidiaries provides a wide range of IT services like software development, capacity planning, business process outsourcing, consulting, enterprise software (ERP), hardware sizing, technology education services, payment processing, etc. TCS’s established software products are TCS MasterCraft & TCS BaNCS.
