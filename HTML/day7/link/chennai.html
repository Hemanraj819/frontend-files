Zoho Corporation is a global, privately held technology company that develops cloud-based business software, operating systems, and hardware tools. It provides a comprehensive suite of more than 55 applications for customer relationship management (CRM), finance, human resources, and workplace collaboration, serving over 150 million users worldwide

Founding: The company was founded in 1996 by Sridhar Vembu and Tony Thomas, originally as AdventNet, Inc., in New Jersey.Headquarters: While its US headquarters is in Austin, Texas, its global headquarters is in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.Ownership: Uniquely for a major tech company of its size, Zoho is completely bootstrapped (privately funded) and has never taken venture capital or gone public.Privacy: Zoho operates without an ad-revenue model across its entire business, ensuring that user data is protected and kept private

4.INFOSYS

Infosys is a global leader in IT services, consulting, and business process outsourcing, headquartered in Bengaluru, India. Founded in 1981, it has grown into a $19.8 billion enterprise with operations spanning over 50 countries and a massive workforce worldwide. The company trades on the NYSE under the ticker INFY.Infosys pioneered the Global Delivery Model and helps enterprises navigate their digital transformations with AI-powered core solutions, cloud services, and agile digital at scale

Infosys was founded by N. R. Narayana Murthy and six other engineers in 1981. Because it is a publicly traded company on the NYSE and NSE, it does not have a single "owner". Rather, its largest shareholders are institutional investors, and its founders (including Murthy and Nandan Nilekani) hold minority stakes.
