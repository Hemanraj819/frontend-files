4.Flipkart

Headquartered in Bengaluru, Flipkart is India's premier homegrown e-commerce marketplace. Founded in October 2007 by former Amazon employees Sachin and Binny Bansal, the company initially started as an online bookstore before expanding into consumer electronics, fashion, and groceries. Walmart acquired a majority stake in 2018, solidifying its dominant position against competitors like Amazon India.

The current Group CEO of Flipkart is Kalyan Krishnamurthy. He has been leading India's leading e-commerce platform since 2018, playing a pivotal role in its immense growth, expansion, and its ongoing transition as a future-proof, AI-first retail ecosystem under Walmart's ownership.
