Tata Consultancy Services(TCS)

About Company

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is a global IT services, consulting, and business solutions leader. Headquartered in Mumbai, it is the flagship company of the Tata Group. Founded in 1968, it is India’s largest company by market capitalization and one of the "Big 4" most valuable IT services brands globally. LinkedIn India ·Tata Consultancy Services +3 TCS operates in 55 countries with over 200 service delivery centers. As a digital transformation partner, it provides services spanning cloud computing, artificial intelligence, cyber security, and enterprise applications. Key recent innovations include the launch of Industrial AI labs with NVIDIA and Gemini Experience Centers with Google Cloud

Services

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) provides end-to-end IT services, business consulting, and cognitive business operations. Key offerings include enterprise AI, cloud migration, cybersecurity, data analytics, and platform solutions across 150+ locations, including major delivery centers in Chennai at Ramanujan IT City (Tharamani), Perungudi, and Velachery
