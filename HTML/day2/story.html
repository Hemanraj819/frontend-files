MS dhoni

Mahendra Singh Dhoni, born July 7, 1981, in Ranchi, is a legendary Indian cricketer. Rising from a modest background, he worked as a ticket collector before pursuing cricket. Renowned as "Captain Cool," he revolutionized Indian cricket, famously leading the team to three ICC titles: the 2007 T20 World Cup, the 2011 ODI World Cup, and the 2013 Champions Trophy.His journey from the small-town streets of Jharkhand to international glory is an inspiring tale of grit, unorthodox technique, and brilliant leadership
