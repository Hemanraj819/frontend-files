About the company

Amazon is a global technology and e-commerce company founded by Jeff Bezos in 1994. It began as an online bookstore and has grown into one of the world's largest online retailers. The company offers a wide range of products and services, including e-commerce, cloud computing through Amazon Web Services (AWS), digital streaming, and artificial intelligence solutions. Amazon's mission is to be Earth's most customer-centric company by providing customers with a wide selection, low prices, fast delivery, and excellent customer service
