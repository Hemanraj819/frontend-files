BHARATHIAR UNIVERSITY

Bharathidasan University, based in Tiruchirappalli, does not have any regular, physical affiliated colleges in Coimbatore. The primary university for regular colleges in the Coimbatore region is Bharathiar University.However, if you are looking for distance education, Bharathidasan University operates authorized Learner Support Centers (LSCs) in Coimbatore for its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE).
