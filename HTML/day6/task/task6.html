My Favorite Actor

Ajith Kumar, popularly known as "Thala", is an iconic Indian superstar whose journey spans over three decades in Tamil cinema. Honored with the Padma Bhushan in 2025, he rose from a self-made mechanic and model to a massive cultural icon renowned for his versatile acting and professional racing career


My favorite place

Renowned globally as "God's Own Country," Kerala is a stunning blend of misty hill stations, lush spice plantations, and golden sand beaches intersected by tranquil backwaters. The state is a top destination for nature lovers, offering tranquil houseboat cruises in Alappuzha, the rolling green tea gardens of Munnar, and the healing arts of Ayurveda.


My favorite food

Biryani is a fragrant, flavorful mixed rice dish featuring long-grain basmati rice and richly spiced meats, seafood, or vegetables. Developed in the royal kitchens of the Indian subcontinent, it is traditionally slow-cooked in a sealed pot (dum) to lock in aromas, and typically served with cooling raita (yogurt sauce) or spicy gravy
